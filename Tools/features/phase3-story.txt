# High Water Mark - phase 3: story, party options and presentation
# Plan this AFTER phase 2 is built and committed:
#   .\claude-batch.ps1 -Features Tools\features\phase3-story.txt -PlanOnly
# The full design is in game-spec.txt at the repo root; each feature names the spec section to read.

1. New game setup (spec SX 07). Choose father or mother, son or daughter, and the child's age (8, 12 or 16).
   Age sets the child's body weight and so their carry limit, their walking speed,
   and whether they are allowed to use the rifle. The world itself is fixed, so there is
   no seed to enter.

2. Prologue walk (spec SX 01, Day 0 18:00). A walk across the north moor on the Ivybridge to Okehampton crossing that
   teaches movement, the paper map and the pack, ending at the old target railway below Rowtor to look at the
   armoured target trolley before pitching camp.

3. The meteor storm set piece (spec SX 01, Day 0 23:00). The one scripted sequence. Golf-ball-sized stones fall
   everywhere and are lethal; a few strikes are metres across. The steel target is completely safe and everywhere
   else is not, so the player must shelter under it. Leaves the craters and strike zones the generator placed.

4. The gas and the surge (spec SX 01, Day 1 03:00 and 07:00). The gas is heavier than air: the player passes out
   and wakes at dawn with a headache and dead skylarks on the grass. Then the sea arrives as three to six crests
   over about eight hours, each a little lower, watched from the target: the player can only look around while it
   fills the West Okement valley and laps at Yes Tor and High Willhays, which become a ridge-island, peaking only
   30 to 50 m below the camp. Control returns when the last crest falls back.

5. No one is coming (spec SX 01). A wind-up or car radio picks up only static, and perhaps a looping automatic
   signal that dies after a few days. No helicopters, broadcasts or relief camps anywhere.

6. Finale: Cheddar Gorge, Priddy and the cellar (spec SX 01, SX 02). The last climb up Cheddar Gorge, stripped to
   bare limestone with rockfalls and the road gone, then the wrecked village of Priddy (stone walls at odd angles,
   roofs gone, wrack across the fields) and the stone cellar door behind the house where the other parent survived.
   Ending screen with journey stats (days, distance, kills and how much was used).

7. Waste has a cost (spec SX 06). Building on the kill-use score: wasting a large animal makes animals warier
   nearby, drops the child's morale, and leaves fewer large animals on the map.

8. Showing the dead and the loss (spec SX 09). Bodies only as pale, soft shapes in the distance or under sheets,
   tarps and wrack; the camera never focuses on them. Dead animals in drifts at the strandline as simple shapes in
   the same grey as the mud. Show the scale of loss through what's missing: empty roads, no sound, a church tower
   standing in the water.
